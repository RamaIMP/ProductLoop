Feeling

Path A: guidance works

The consumer taps That helped. The report is marked resolved. It still counts as a field signal, because the complaint happened even though the fix was easy.

Path B: not fixed, or unsafe

The consumer taps Still not fixed, or reports heat or damage. The app tells them to stop use and shows nearby authorised service centres. The record is flagged higher priority for the manufacturer.
The loop closes: Consumer complaint → Quality signal → Manufacturer investigation → Better products Consumers who report get an answer now. Later buyers get a better product.
